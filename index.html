About

Nails are my little mirror

I started doing nails in 2021, first on my cousin before her college farewell. She would not stop showing her hands to everyone, and that is when I thought, maybe this can be my work.

Five years later I still enjoy the first moment a client looks at her hands and smiles. I named my studio nailsandmirror because good nails should make you feel like yourself, only shinier.

  • I come to your home, no travel, no waiting
  • Fresh, clean tools for every client
  • Soft gel, cat eye and normal polish, you choose
  • I match the design from your reference photo
  • Open 9am to 10pm, even after office hours
